The Minas Name Tag Ritual

A simple post-walk ritual that turns first-time visitors into recognized neighborhood regulars.

1

01 · ARRIVAL

Finish the walk

You and your dog finish your walk at Boston Common and head to Minas.
2

02 · FIRST VISIT

Learn their name

We ask your dog’s name and save it to your profile.
3

03 · THE RITUAL

Create the tag

While your coffee is made, we create a Minas name tag and a treat for your pup.
4

04 · RETURN VISIT

Part of the Community

On each visit, we greet your dog by name. You get your coffee, and your dog gets a treat. You both enjoy a little reward after your long walk.
5

05 · A MINAS REGULAR

Join the wall

After 5 visits, your dog earns a spot on the Minas Regulars wall. You settle in and rest as you drink your coffee. While you rest, your dog either stays by your side and relaxes or goes into the designated dog area to play with the other dogs.
Better coffee. Better company. · Right next to Boston Common.
